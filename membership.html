The Process

How to Join

Membership is by invitation and application. The committee meets quarterly to review all applications. The process typically takes 8–12 weeks.

01

Find a Proposer

You must be proposed and seconded by two current members in good standing who have been members for at least three years.

02

Submit Application

Complete the membership application form, provide your proposer and seconder details, and submit two references from your profession.

03

Committee Review

Your application is reviewed by the Membership Committee. You may be invited for an informal meeting with the President or Secretary.

04

Welcome to the Club

Upon approval, you will receive your membership certificate, club tie or sash, and an invitation to the next new members' cocktail evening.
