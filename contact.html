The committee meets quarterly (January, April, July, October) to review applications. From the time you submit a complete application — with proposer, seconder, and references — the process typically takes 8–12 weeks. Yes. Social members may bring up to 4 guests per month; Playing members up to 8; Patron Royale members enjoy unlimited guest privileges. Guests must be signed in at the front desk and are the responsibility of the inviting member at all times.

The Grand Pavilion is available for private events — weddings, anniversaries, corporate gala dinners, and product launches — for both members and non-members. Capacity ranges from 80 (seated, intimate) to 600 (standing cocktail). All event catering is handled exclusively by our in-house kitchen. We are typically booked 10–14 months in advance for premium dates.

Our coaches have taught complete beginners who have gone on to earn international handicaps. Weekend intensive clinics are available for prospective members who wish to try before applying.

Patron Royale members enjoy reciprocal playing and social privileges at 12 partner clubs worldwide, including the Hurlingham Club (London), Palermo Polo Club (Buenos Aires), and the Dubai Polo & Equestrian Club. A full list of reciprocal clubs is available from the Membership Office.
