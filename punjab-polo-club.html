Our Club

A New Era of
Equestrian Excellence

Nestled in the lush foothills of Siswan, New Chandigarh, the Punjab Polo Club is a bold new chapter in North India's polo legacy. Our world-class grounds span 200 acres of pristine manicured turf — purpose-built for those who live the sport of kings at the highest level.

Designed for modern royalty and seasoned equestrians alike, the club brings together the finest facilities, elite coaching, and a community united by a shared passion for horsemanship, camaraderie, and the timeless allure of polo.

World-Class Grounds
20 acres of pristine manicured turf and polo grounds.
World-Class Stables
10+ elite polo horses of the finest lineage.
Expert Coaching
International coaches with 30-goal combined handicap.
Luxury Clubhouse
Award-winning dining and event facilities.
