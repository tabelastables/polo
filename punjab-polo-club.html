Our Essence

A New Era Carved
in Turf

Nestled in the lush foothills of Siswan, New Chandigarh, Punjab Polo Club is a bold new chapter in North India's polo legacy. Our world-class grounds span 20 acres of pristine manicured turf — purpose-built for those who live the sport of kings at the highest level.

Designed for modern enthusiasts and seasoned equestrians alike, the club brings together elite coaching, championship polo horses, and a community united by a shared passion for horsemanship and the timeless allure of polo.
