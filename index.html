Our Story

A Century of
Equestrian Excellence

Founded in 1898 under the patronage of the Maharaja of Patiala, the Punjab Polo Club stands as one of the oldest and most storied polo institutions in Asia. Our grounds in Ludhiana sprawl across 200 acres of manicured turf, where the sport of kings has been played for over a century.

From British colonial officers to modern-day royalty, our membership has always represented the pinnacle of society — united by a shared passion for horsemanship, camaraderie, and the timeless allure of polo.

Heritage Grounds
200 acres of meticulously maintained turf, two championship maidans.
World-Class Stables
State-of-the-art facilities housing over 80 ponies of Argentine lineage.
Expert Coaching
International coaches with combined handicaps of 30 goals.
Luxury Clubhouse
Award-winning dining, private lounges, and event facilities.
